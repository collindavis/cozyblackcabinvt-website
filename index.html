Your Vermont getaway awaits

Cozy, contemporary, and quietly off the grid of the everyday.

Escape to a log cabin that feels modern and warm at once — black timber outside, soft light and contemporary comfort within.

Centrally located within a 45-minute drive of Vermont's finest skiing. A 10-minute walk to the Brownsville Butcher & Pantry and Ascutney Outdoors, where you can bike, hike, ski, and tube. Or simply stay home: soak in the hot tub, roast marshmallows at the fire pit, and curl up with blankets and games by the fire.

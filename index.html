Your Vermont getaway awaits

Cozy, contemporary, and quietly off the grid of the everyday.

Escape to a log cabin that feels modern and warm at once — black timber outside, soft light and contemporary comfort within.

Centrally located within a 45-minute drive of Vermont's finest skiing. A 10-minute walk to the Brownsville Butcher & Pantry and Ascutney Outdoors, where you can bike, hike, ski, and tube. Or simply stay home: soak in the hot tub, roast marshmallows at the fire pit, and curl up with blankets and games by the fire.

Four seasons at the cabin

There's no wrong time to come up.

Vermont rewards every season differently. Here's what each one looks like from the deck.

Winter

Ski & soak

45 minutes to Killington, Okemo & Saskadena Six. Tube at Ascutney, then thaw in the hot tub.

Spring

Quiet & green

Mud-season calm, maple everything, and trails waking back up. The locals' favorite secret.

Summer

Bike & hike

Mountain biking and hiking right out the door at Ascutney Outdoors, then dinner at the Butcher.

Fall

Peak foliage

Front-row seats to New England's headline act. Book early — leaf-peeper weekends fill fast.

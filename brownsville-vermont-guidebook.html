Eat & drink

Where we eat near the cabin.

Our favorites within about 30 minutes of the property. Popular spots fill up, so reserve ahead when you can.

  • Breakfast& cafe
    Brownsville Butcher & PantryGreat breakfast and cafe food, plus a grocery of local produce and meat. It gets busy around 10–10:30am and breakfast stops at 10:45, so beat the rush. Hours vary by season.
  • Breakfastworth the wait
    The Hartland DinerOur favorite breakfast spot. It's a small space and you usually wait for a table, but it's worth it.
  • Dinnerreserve
    Simon PearceA restaurant inside a glassblowing mill, with an Ottauquechee River view and a retail shop next door. Book reservations early.
  • Dinnerreserve
    Skunk Hollow TavernA tiny 1700s inn run by the chef and the bartender. Strong drinks, delicious food, and it books up fast. Call ahead and leave a message to reserve.
  • Finedining
    Mangalitsa & SantéMangalitsa is a favorite for fine dining, with amazing food and service. Santé has the same owner and a rotating menu of locally grown, locally sourced food.
  • Pizza& pub
    Windsor StationA local staple in an old train station. Reserve ahead, especially on weekends. Be there around 5:30–6pm to feel the Amtrak Vermonter rumble past. For takeout, order ahead online.
  • Pizza& arcade
    Pizza Chef WindsorOver 20 years in Windsor, with a dive-bar corner, an arcade for kids and trivia every Thursday at 6pm.
  • Diner& ice cream
    Frazer's PlaceA former A&W, now a family-owned diner serving classic diner food, plus ice cream in summer.
Breweries & Woodstock

Beer, burgers and a pretty main street.

More places our guests love, from craft breweries to Woodstock's village restaurants.

  • BreweryWindsor
    Harpoon Brewery Taproom & Beer GardenCraft beer and bar food, with a big outdoor area in warm months (lawn games, a playground, live music). The plaza also has the Path of Life Sculpture Garden.
  • BreweryQuechee
    Long Trail Brewing CompanyOne of the original craft breweries that put Vermont on the map. Live music in summer and a great spot by the river. Reserve in advance.
  • Taproomtacos
    Trail Break Taps + TacosFantastic food and drinks, though hours vary. Check their website.
  • PizzaWoodstock
    The Ransom TavernInside the Kedron Valley Inn in South Woodstock, with great drinks and wood-fired pizza. Reservations are hard to get and hours are limited, so check their website.
  • BurgersWoodstock
    Worthy KitchenAwesome burgers and beers on tap.
  • CocktailsWoodstock
    Au ComptoirA great little craft cocktail bar just off Main Street.
  • SnacksWoodstock
    The White Cottage Snack BarA diner-style staple right on the river for many years. Good ice cream, too.
  • MarketWoodstock
    Woodstock Farmers' MarketLocal produce, cheese, beer, coffee, sandwiches, to-go meals and more.
  • CafeWoodstock
    Mon Vert CafeLocal eats and treats in the heart of Woodstock.
  • PubWoodstock
    On the River InnA hidden gem for food and drinks, attached to the inn.
  • DinnerClaremont
    The Common ManA huge menu in a restored mill right on the river. The food is good, but service can be slow when it's busy, so try an earlier dinner.
Nearby towns

Villages worth a day trip.

  • 20 mindrive
    WoodstockRanked one of the best Christmas towns by Country Living Magazine. Charming in any season, with dining, shopping and sightseeing.
  • NorthI-91
    White River JunctionOn the rise, with great dining, an arts center and River Roost Brewery. Worth the drive up I-91.
  • Allin one

Outdoors & skiing

Mountains, trails and ski areas.

Mount Ascutney is the backyard. Beyond it, a few of New England's best ski areas are a day trip away.

  • 5 mindrive
    Ascutney OutdoorsA non-profit, volunteer-run outdoor center offering hiking, mountain biking, skiing and snow tubing. It relies on natural snow in winter, so check their website or Facebook page for current hours and conditions.
  • Seasonalmid-May–Oct
    Mt Ascutney State ParkHome to the auto road, hiking and camping, open mid-May to mid-October.
  • 30 mindrive
    Saskadena SixFormerly Suicide Six. A small local mountain in Pomfret that's great for family skiing.
  • 40 mindrive
    Okemo Mountain ResortMore terrain and the dining and shopping of Ludlow, with bigger crowds. Okemo is owned by Vail Resorts.
  • Biggestin the East
    Killington ResortThe “Beast of the East” has the most total terrain in the Northeast, with trails for every level and lots of dining and nightlife in town. A favorite run is Great Eastern from the top of K1 to the bottom of the Skyeship, when conditions allow.
  • Lake& ski
    Sunapee MountainSki the mountain in winter, then head to Sunapee Lake and Silver Lake when it's warm.
  • Wheels& farms
    ATV VermontA guest-recommended option for ATV adventures. Check their website for details.

Trails from the driveway

Ride it, hike it, then look at it.

Mount Ascutney stands alone above the Connecticut River valley, so the summit views reach all the way to the Whites on a clear day. The Ascutney Trails network wraps its western base, right where the cabin sits.

  • 5 mindrive
    Ascutney Outdoors CenterThe hub for mountain biking, with beginner to expert singletrack and the only trailhead that allows e-bikes.
  • 45+miles
    Ascutney Trails singletrackHand-built flow trails, rock gardens and berms. Pick up the paper trail map at the Butcher & Pantry.
  • 3.2miles
    Brownsville Trail to the summitClimb past the old Norcross granite quarry to Brownsville Rock and the summit observation tower.
  • 0.7miles
    The easy way upDrive the summit road and take the short Slab Trail to the tower.

Good to know

Planning a trail trip.

  • May–Oct
    Riding seasonThe bike trails are usually at their best from late spring through October. Check Trailforks for conditions.
  • Tuenights
    Group ridesThe Ascutney Trails Association runs weekly group rides from May through October.
  • Eatnearby
    Refuel down the roadThe Brownsville Butcher & Pantry is a short walk away, for coffee, sandwiches and dinner.
  • Restday
    Rest-day optionsWoodstock is 25 minutes away and Quechee about 30.

Pick your mountain

Five ski areas, one home base.

Most ski houses put you at the foot of one resort. The cabin sits in the middle of central Vermont's ski country, so you can chase the snow: big terrain at Killington, corduroy at Okemo, and a quiet local hill for the kids, all from the same driveway.

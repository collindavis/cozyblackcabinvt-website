Leaf-peeping drives

The best color is close to home.

Every photo on this site was taken in the fall, and the view from the deck is the main event. When you want to get out, the classic foliage drives start a few minutes from the driveway.

  • 10 mindrive
    Mount Ascutney summit roadA paved 3.7-mile road climbs almost to the top, with a short hike to the summit tower and views across Vermont and New Hampshire.
  • 15 mindrive
    Cornish–Windsor Covered BridgeCross the Connecticut River on one of America's longest covered bridges.
  • 25 mindrive
    WoodstockVermont's postcard village, with the green, Billings Farm and the Marsh-Billings-Rockefeller trails.
  • 30 mindrive
    Quechee Gorge & Simon PearceVermont's "Little Grand Canyon", then lunch over the waterfall at Simon Pearce.

Planning your trip

Timing the color.

  • PeakOct
    Early to mid-OctoberColor around Mount Ascutney usually peaks in early to mid-October, a little later than northern Vermont.
  • Bookearly
    October weekends go firstIndigenous Peoples' Day weekend and the first half of October book up early. Midweek stays are quieter and easier to find.
  • Eatnearby
    Walk to breakfast, lunch or dinnerThe Brownsville Butcher & Pantry is a short walk down the road.
  • Tastelocal
    Apple and cider seasonOrchards and farm stands around the Upper Valley sell apples, cider and cider donuts all fall.
